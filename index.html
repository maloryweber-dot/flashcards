How scheduling works

SM-2 style. New cards pass 1-min and 10-min learning steps, then graduate to 1 day. Good multiplies the interval by the card's ease (starts 2.5); Easy grows it more; Hard grows it slightly and lowers ease; Again sends it back to relearning. Say the answer out loud before revealing. Keys: Space = reveal / Good, 1-4 = Again / Hard / Good / Easy.
